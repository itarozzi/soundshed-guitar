Preset Switching

Lets the preset you just left keep ringing out over the new one. Its input fades away, and its delay and reverb state is kept for the selected number of bars before fading out. Bars are counted at the current tempo, and each preset still ringing costs CPU. Switching again may end an older tail sooner.
